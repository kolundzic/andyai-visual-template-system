# AVTS-004 — Multilingual Executive Poster

## Category
Multilingual Academic Poster

## Purpose
Create polished EN/SR/JP educational or executive posters with locked text and high readability.

## Best For
- partner presentations
- business explainers
- course posters
- international materials
- product positioning

## Style Family
Gold Authority Layer

## Recommended Ratio
4:5

## Prompt

```text
Generate a multilingual executive academic poster about [topic].

Purpose:
Explain the concept clearly for an international professional audience.

Languages:
[English + Serbian] or [English + Japanese]

Main visual:
[one strong symbolic hero visual]

Include exact text:
Title EN: [title]
Title SR/JP: [translated title]
Subtitle EN: [subtitle]
Subtitle SR/JP: [translated subtitle]
Key Point 1 EN: [text]
Key Point 1 SR/JP: [text]
Key Point 2 EN: [text]
Key Point 2 SR/JP: [text]
Key Point 3 EN: [text]
Key Point 3 SR/JP: [text]

Layout:
Executive poster with strong hierarchy, generous spacing, bilingual alignment, clear sections.

Style:
AndyAI academic sci-fi, Gold Authority Layer, elegant, premium, Japanese-inspired precision.

Color direction:
Ink black or deep navy background, pearl white text, warm gold highlights, soft cyan dividers.

Text instruction:
Render all text exactly. No extra words. No spelling changes. No invented characters.

Avoid:
distorted text, too-small bilingual text, crowded layout, fake logos, watermark, random decorative symbols.
```

## Project Mapping
- Agency OS
- Finance Engine
- Human Attention
- AndyAI Academy
- Partner presentations

## Quality Gate
Bilingual text must remain clear, balanced, and professional.
