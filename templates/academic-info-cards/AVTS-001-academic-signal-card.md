# AVTS-001 — Academic Signal Card

## Category
Academic Info Card / Signal Summary

## Purpose
Transform a new idea, article, video, screenshot, or market signal into a clean educational card.

## Best For
- signal summaries
- mini frameworks
- rules
- 5-step explainers
- social educational posts
- Notion-ready visual summaries

## Style Family
Japanese Future Academic

## Recommended Ratio
4:5

## Prompt

```text
Generate an academic signal card for [topic].

Purpose:
Explain the core signal clearly to beginners and technical readers.

Audience:
[beginner / technical / business / academic]

Main idea:
[one sentence summary of the signal]

Include exact text:
Title: [short title]
Subtitle: [short explanatory subtitle]
Signal: [what happened]
Why it matters: [why it matters]
AndyAI angle: [how AndyAI interprets it]
Practical takeaway: [what to do next]

Layout:
Vertical 4:5 card with a strong title, one central symbolic visual, and 4–6 clean information blocks.

Style:
AndyAI academic sci-fi, Japanese Future Academic, calm futuristic, precise, premium educational design.

Color direction:
Midnight navy background, pearl white text, jade green for verified insight, soft cyan for data/structure, warm gold for priority.

Avoid:
tiny text, crowded sections, random icons, fake logos, unreadable glow, generic AI stock look, watermark.
```

## Project Mapping
- Human Attention
- RAG Ingestion Factory
- Knowledge Factory
- AndyAI Academy
- Visual Canon

## Quality Gate
The card must teach one idea in under 20 seconds.
